Über dieses Buch

In 1960, fourteen African nations raised their flags over empty parliament buildings. The French tricolor came down. The colonial administrators boarded planes for Paris. But the systems that had governed them for a century did not leave. They simply changed form. In this explosive exposé, Salomon Owona reveals how neocolonialism has evolved from gunboats and resource extraction to a sophisticated digital empire run by algorithms. Drawing on three powerful chapters—'The Empire That Never Left,' 'The Algorithmic Governor,' and 'Breaking the Algorithmic Chain'—Owona shows how machine learning and data control now dictate the fate of entire nations, from credit scores to election outcomes. Yet resistance is rising. Across Africa, a new generation of digital activists, coders, and thinkers is fighting back, building pathways to sovereignty in the age of AI. This is a must-read for anyone who wants to understand the true architecture of power in the 21st century—and how to dismantle it.
